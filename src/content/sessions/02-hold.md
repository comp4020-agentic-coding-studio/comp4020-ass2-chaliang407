Last week's press was instant — it happened, and it was over. This week adds
duration: a continuous value, measured from the moment of press to right now,
that exists *before* release. Nothing is decided yet by letting go; that's
next week. This week is only about isolating the signal.

## Before the session

Bring one of your week 1 prototypes. You'll be adding to it, not starting
over.

## In the session

Short demo of hold-duration as a readout (a bar filling, a number climbing, a
pitch rising), then build time extending your own prototype.

## Studio exercise

Take a week 1 prototype and add a visible readout driven purely by how long
the button has been held. No release logic — the outcome doesn't change when
you let go, only the readout while you're still holding.

## By the end

Hold-duration now reads as a continuous channel layered on top of last
week's discrete event — not a new kind of input, just a new way of reading
the old one.
