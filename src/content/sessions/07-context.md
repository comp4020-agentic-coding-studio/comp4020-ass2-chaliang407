Nothing new is measured this week — instead, a piece of state decides which
of the earlier meanings currently applies. This is the first week that
reinterprets rather than adds: the same press can trigger week 3's
release-timing in one mode and read as part of week 5's sequence in another,
switched by something like week 6's momentum crossing a threshold.

## Before the session

Bring two earlier prototypes whose mechanics you want to combine (release
and momentum work well together, but any pairing is fair game).

## In the session

Demo of one button behaving two different ways depending on a mode flag,
then build time combining your own two prototypes behind it.

## Studio exercise

Pick two earlier mechanics and put them behind a single button, switched by
one piece of state you control (a threshold, a phase, a toggled flag). A
player who has never seen either prototype before should be able to tell
which mode is active from what's on screen — not by trial and error.

This week's exercise stops at two mechanics on purpose — that's enough to
prove the switch is legible. The Context Switch assessment later asks for
the same technique at three mechanics or more, on your own, without a studio
session to build it in first. Treat today's prototype as the technique
you'll need to extend independently, not as the ceiling of what it can do.

## By the end

You've confirmed that "one button" was never really a limit on how many
things a press can mean — only on how many things can be pressed at once.
