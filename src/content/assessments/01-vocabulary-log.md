> Show your working: a small, playable proof for each of the first six
> primitives, in the order you built them.

This isn't six polished games — it's six small proofs, submitted as they're
made rather than assembled at the deadline. The mark isn't for how much each
one does; it's for whether each one demonstrates exactly its week's addition
to the vocabulary and nothing more.

## What you submit

A folder or repo of six runnable prototypes, one per week, each named for
the primitive it demonstrates (press, hold, release, rhythm, sequence,
momentum). A one-line note per prototype naming which earlier one it reuses,
if any.
