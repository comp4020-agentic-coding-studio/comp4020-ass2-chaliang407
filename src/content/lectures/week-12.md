No new mechanic this week, on purpose. Every primitive from press through
scanning is on the table; the skill being tested is deciding which ones a
single game needs and cutting the rest. A game that crams in all eleven
earlier weeks is not the strongest response to this brief — it's usually the
one that hasn't decided what it's about.

## Outline

- why restraint, not addition, is this week's actual subject
- reviewing the full vocabulary as a set of choices, not a checklist to
  complete
- what a scope decision has to state to count as deliberate rather than
  accidental
