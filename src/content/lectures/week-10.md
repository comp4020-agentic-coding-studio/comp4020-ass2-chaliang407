Two players, one button each, is a much bigger space than "one button
total." The interesting design work is making each player's press change
meaning in relation to the other's — which needs week 4's rhythm, for
reading someone else's timing, and week 7's shared context, now read by two
input streams instead of one.

## Outline

- concurrent input streams: two single bits interacting through shared state
- why running two single-player prototypes side by side isn't the same
  thing
- the "unplug one player" test for whether the dependency is real
