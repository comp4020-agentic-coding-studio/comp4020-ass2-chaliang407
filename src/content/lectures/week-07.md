This is the first week that reinterprets the vocabulary rather than adding to
it. A piece of state — a mode, a phase, a threshold like week 6's momentum
crossing a line — decides which of the earlier meanings currently applies to
a press. Everything built in weeks 1–6 becomes conditional from here on.

## Outline

- mode-dependent meaning: one input, several possible interpretations
- why "one button" was never a limit on how much a press can mean
- making a mode switch legible to a player who's never seen it before
