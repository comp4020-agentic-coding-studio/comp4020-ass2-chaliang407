This week is the semester's first consolidation rather than a new axis: short
press and long press (weeks 2–3) become two distinguishable symbols, and
week 4's timing windows are what let a game tell where one symbol ends and
the next begins. A vocabulary, in other words, is what you get from combining
primitives under a shared timing rule.

## Outline

- symbols as named, distinguishable press-shapes, not raw signals
- segmentation: how timing windows turn a stream of presses into a sequence
- why a 3–5 symbol alphabet is already enough to be interesting
